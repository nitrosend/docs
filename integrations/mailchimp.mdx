# Mailchimp

Connect Mailchimp to migrate your existing audiences and members into Nitrosend.

## What syncs

The Mailchimp connector imports:

- Audiences as Nitrosend contact lists
- Audience members as contacts
- Email address, first name, last name, and common merge fields
- Audience membership
- Mailchimp source metadata on the contact

Mailchimp campaign history, templates, and reports are not migrated.

## Consent handling

Each member's Mailchimp status decides their email subscription in Nitrosend:

| Mailchimp status | In Nitrosend |
| --- | --- |
| `subscribed` | Subscribed to email |
| `unsubscribed` | Unsubscribed from marketing email |
| `cleaned` | Not subscribed, and the address is blocked from all email in this brand as a hard bounce |
| `pending`, `transactional`, archived or unknown | Not subscribed. This is not an unsubscribe. |

A person can be in several audiences. Nitrosend reads all of them and makes one decision per contact:

- An unsubscribe in any audience wins. The contact is unsubscribed in Nitrosend, even if another audience has them as `subscribed`.
- A `pending`, `transactional` or archived status in one audience does not unsubscribe someone who is `subscribed` in another.
- Someone who was `pending` and later confirms in Mailchimp becomes subscribed on a later sync, as long as they have not opted out or been suppressed in Nitrosend.

Anyone already unsubscribed or blocked in Nitrosend stays that way when Mailchimp syncs again.

Moving off Mailchimp for good? Import each audience's unsubscribed and cleaned export files before you connect. The [migration guide](/guides/migrating-to-nitrosend#mailchimp) has the steps.

## Connect Mailchimp

1. Go to **Integrations** in Nitrosend.
2. Choose **Mailchimp**.
3. Click **Connect** and complete the Mailchimp OAuth flow.
4. Return to Nitrosend and watch the sync progress.

The sync runs in the background. You can leave the page while it imports contacts and return to the integration detail page to check progress.

## After import

After the sync completes:

- Review imported lists in **Contacts > Lists** ([app.nitrosend.com/my/contact-lists](https://app.nitrosend.com/my/contact-lists)).
- Use [segments](/concepts/segments) to build dynamic audiences from Mailchimp lists, tags, merge fields, and engagement data.
- Send only to contacts who are subscribed in Nitrosend.

<Warning>
  Mailchimp sync is a contact and audience migration path. It does not automatically send campaigns or activate automations.
</Warning>
