# Shopify

Connect your Shopify store to import customers into Nitrosend and receive commerce activity you can use in segments and automation flows. If the store is moving from another email tool, import that tool's opt-outs first, as the [migration FAQ](/faq#contacts-and-migration) describes.

## Customer import

The Shopify connector imports:

- Shopify customers as Nitrosend contacts (email and phone where present)
- Shopify source metadata on the contact

Customers are imported as contacts only — Shopify sync does not create contact lists. Use [segments](/concepts/segments) to build audiences from imported customers and their activity.

## Consent handling

Nitrosend honours the marketing consent recorded in Shopify: customers who opted in to email or SMS marketing in your store are imported as subscribed on that channel; everyone else is imported **unsubscribed by default**.

## Store event webhooks

When you connect, Nitrosend automatically registers webhooks on your store for order creation and payment, refunds, product creation/updates/deletion, and customer creation/updates. Every webhook is verified against Shopify's HMAC signature before it is processed. That activity flows into your contacts' event history, where segments and flow triggers can use it. Nitrosend also handles Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks (customer data requests and redaction).

## Storefront Web Pixel (optional)

For storefront browsing activity, the integration page shows a read-only **Web Pixel collector key**. Use this public key as the `collectorKey` setting in the Shopify Web Pixel extension to send storefront events to Nitrosend.

## Best sellers and new arrivals in your emails

Once your store is connected, a product grid can fill itself from your catalog. In the email editor, open the grid's **Product content** and choose **Best sellers** or **New arrivals** and how many to show. Best sellers ranks your recent paid orders, and New arrivals shows your newest active products.

Nitrosend fills the grid each time you save the email, so the preview, the test and the send review show exactly what will go out. Emails in a live flow also refresh once a day when your products change; with Safe Mode on, the refresh waits for you to publish it, and a flow with a change waiting to be published or a random split is skipped. If a daily refresh finds no products, the email keeps the products it had. An email saved with an empty grid can't be sent until you save it again with products or switch the grid to products you choose.

## Connect Shopify

1. Go to **Integrations** in Nitrosend.
2. Choose **Shopify** and enter your store domain.
3. Click **Connect** and complete the Shopify OAuth flow.
4. Return to Nitrosend and run **Sync customers**.

The sync runs in the background — the sync progress tray keeps you updated anywhere in the dashboard, or return to the integration detail page for details. Store webhooks keep commerce activity flowing after the initial sync.

## After import

- Review imported customers in **Contacts**.
- Use [segments](/concepts/segments) to build audiences from customer fields and commerce events.
- Build [flows](/concepts/flows) triggered by store activity.
- Send only to contacts who are subscribed in Nitrosend.

<Warning>
  Shopify sync imports customers and receives store events. It does not migrate your Shopify email templates or discount codes, and it does not write data back to Shopify.
</Warning>
