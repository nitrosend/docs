# Shopify

Connect your Shopify store to import customers into Nitrosend and receive commerce activity you can use in segments and automation flows. If the store is moving from another email tool, import that tool's opt-outs first, as the [migration FAQ](/faq#contacts-and-migration) describes.

## Customer import

The Shopify connector imports:

- Shopify customers as Nitrosend contacts (email and phone where present)
- Shopify source metadata on the contact

Customers are imported as contacts only — Shopify sync does not create contact lists. Use [segments](/concepts/segments) to build audiences from imported customers and their activity.

## Consent handling

Nitrosend follows the marketing consent recorded in Shopify, for email and SMS separately:

| In Shopify | In Nitrosend |
| --- | --- |
| Subscribed to email or SMS marketing | Subscribed on that channel |
| Unsubscribed | Unsubscribed on that channel, including a customer who was subscribed in Nitrosend before |
| Not subscribed, pending or anything else | A new contact is not subscribed. This is not an unsubscribe, so a contact who is already subscribed stays subscribed. |

- **Consent stays in step after the first sync.** Shopify tells Nitrosend when a customer's marketing consent changes, so an unsubscribe in your store reaches Nitrosend without a manual sync.
- **Buying is not subscribing.** A customer first seen on an order or an abandoned checkout is not subscribed unless Shopify says they opted in. If they opt in at checkout, they become subscribed when Shopify reports it.
- **The newest choice wins.** A Shopify unsubscribe dated before a later opt-in the customer gave you directly in Nitrosend does not undo that opt-in.
- **Opt-outs hold.** Anyone who unsubscribed or was suppressed in Nitrosend stays that way, whatever Shopify says later.

Moving from Shopify Email? Import your unsubscribed, bounced and spam segments before you connect. The [migration guide](/guides/migrating-to-nitrosend#shopify) has the steps.

## Store event webhooks

When you connect, Nitrosend automatically registers webhooks on your store for order creation and payment, refunds, product creation/updates/deletion, and customer creation/updates. It also registers for changes to a customer's email or SMS marketing consent, which update the contact's subscription. Every webhook is verified against Shopify's HMAC signature before it is processed. Order, payment and refund activity flows into your contacts' event history, where segments and flow triggers can use it. Product webhooks keep your catalog current. Nitrosend also handles Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks (customer data requests and redaction).

## Storefront Web Pixel (optional)

For storefront browsing activity, the integration page shows a read-only **Web Pixel collector key**. Use this public key as the `collectorKey` setting in the Shopify Web Pixel extension to send storefront events to Nitrosend.

## Connect Shopify

1. Go to **Integrations** in Nitrosend.
2. Choose **Shopify** and enter your store domain.
3. Click **Connect** and complete the Shopify OAuth flow.
4. Return to Nitrosend and run **Sync customers**.

The sync runs in the background — the sync progress tray keeps you updated anywhere in the dashboard, or return to the integration detail page for details. Store webhooks keep commerce activity flowing after the initial sync.

## After import

- Review imported customers in **Contacts**.
- Use [segments](/concepts/segments) to build audiences from customer fields and commerce events.
- Build [flows](/concepts/flows) triggered by store activity.
- Send only to contacts who are subscribed in Nitrosend.

<Warning>
  Shopify sync imports customers and receives store events. It does not migrate your Shopify email templates or discount codes, and it does not write data back to Shopify.
</Warning>
