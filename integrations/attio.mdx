# Attio

Connect Attio to keep people and list membership from your CRM synchronized with Nitrosend.

## What syncs

The Attio connector imports:

- Attio people as Nitrosend contacts (email and phone where present)
- Attio list membership as Nitrosend list membership
- Attio source metadata on the contact

Attio notes, tasks, deals, and custom objects are not imported.

## Consent handling

Attio is a CRM, not a marketing-consent system, so Nitrosend imports Attio people conservatively: email and phone channels are **unsubscribed by default**. Keep the contact data without widening your sendable audience — opt contacts in through your own consent flow before sending marketing email.

## Connect Attio

1. Go to **Integrations** in Nitrosend.
2. Choose **Attio**.
3. Click **Connect** and complete the Attio OAuth flow.
4. Return to Nitrosend and run **Sync contacts**.

The sync runs in the background. You can leave the page while it imports people and list membership, and return to the integration detail page to check progress. Run **Sync contacts** again any time to refresh.

## After import

- Review imported lists in **Contacts > Lists**.
- Use [segments](/concepts/segments) to build audiences from Attio lists and contact fields.
- Send only to contacts who are subscribed in Nitrosend.

<Warning>
  Attio sync imports people and list membership. It does not send campaigns, activate automations, or write data back to Attio.
</Warning>
