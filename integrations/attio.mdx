# Attio

Connect Attio to keep people and list membership from your CRM synchronized with Nitrosend.

## What syncs

The Attio connector imports:

- Attio people as Nitrosend contacts (email and phone where present)
- Attio list membership as Nitrosend list membership
- Attio source metadata on the contact

Attio notes, tasks, deals, and custom objects are not imported.

## Marketing consent

Attio is a CRM, and Nitrosend does not read marketing consent from it. So new contacts from Attio arrive **not subscribed** to email until an account owner or admin confirms that the people in this connection agreed to receive marketing email from you.

To confirm, open Attio under **Integrations**, find **Marketing consent**, choose **Review and confirm**, tick the statement and choose **Confirm consent**.

- **After you confirm,** new contacts from Attio arrive subscribed to email, and contacts this connection was holding as not subscribed are subscribed in the background. Confirming covers email only. It does not subscribe anyone to SMS.
- **Opt-outs still win.** Anyone who unsubscribed or was suppressed stays not subscribed, whatever you confirm.
- **It covers one Attio workspace.** Nitrosend records who confirmed and when. Reconnecting to a different Attio workspace clears the confirmation.
- **You can withdraw it.** Withdrawing only affects contacts that arrive afterward. Anyone already subscribed stays subscribed.

Confirming or withdrawing does not change contacts that were in Nitrosend before the connection began holding new ones. Until you confirm, you can still use Attio people in segments and send them transactional email.

## Connect Attio

1. Go to **Integrations** in Nitrosend.
2. Choose **Attio**.
3. Click **Connect** and complete the Attio OAuth flow.
4. Return to Nitrosend and run **Sync contacts**.

The sync runs in the background. The sync progress tray keeps you updated anywhere in the dashboard, or return to the integration detail page for details. Run **Sync contacts** again any time to refresh.

## After import

- Review imported lists in **Contacts > Lists** ([app.nitrosend.com/my/contact-lists](https://app.nitrosend.com/my/contact-lists)).
- Use [segments](/concepts/segments) to build audiences from Attio lists and contact fields.
- Send only to contacts who are subscribed in Nitrosend.

<Warning>
  Attio sync imports people and list membership. It does not send campaigns, activate automations, or write data back to Attio.
</Warning>
