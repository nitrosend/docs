# HubSpot

Connect HubSpot to keep CRM contacts and lists synchronized with Nitrosend.

## What syncs

The HubSpot connector imports:

- HubSpot contacts as Nitrosend contacts
- HubSpot lists as Nitrosend list membership
- HubSpot source metadata on the contact

HubSpot deals, companies, tickets, and marketing assets are not imported.

## Consent handling

Nitrosend imports HubSpot contacts conservatively: the email channel is **unsubscribed by default**. Keep the CRM record without widening your sendable audience — opt contacts in through your own consent flow before sending marketing email.

## Connect HubSpot

1. Go to **Integrations** in Nitrosend.
2. Choose **HubSpot**.
3. Click **Connect** and complete the HubSpot OAuth flow.
4. Return to Nitrosend and run **Sync contacts**.

The sync runs in the background. You can leave the page while it imports contacts and lists, and return to the integration detail page to check progress. Run **Sync contacts** again any time to refresh.

## After import

- Review imported lists in **Contacts > Lists**.
- Use [segments](/concepts/segments) to build audiences from HubSpot lists and contact fields.
- Send only to contacts who are subscribed in Nitrosend.

<Warning>
  HubSpot sync imports contacts and lists. It does not send campaigns, activate automations, or write data back to HubSpot.
</Warning>
