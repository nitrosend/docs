# HubSpot

Connect HubSpot to keep CRM contacts and lists synchronized with Nitrosend.

## What syncs

The HubSpot connector imports:

- HubSpot contacts as Nitrosend contacts
- HubSpot lists as Nitrosend list membership
- HubSpot source metadata on the contact

HubSpot deals, companies, tickets, and marketing assets are not imported.

## Marketing consent

A CRM holds many people who never asked for marketing email. So new contacts from HubSpot arrive **not subscribed** to email until an account owner or admin confirms that the people in this connection agreed to receive marketing email from you.

To confirm, open HubSpot under **Integrations**, find **Marketing consent**, choose **Review and confirm**, tick the statement and choose **Confirm consent**.

- **After you confirm,** new contacts from HubSpot arrive subscribed, and contacts that were held as not subscribed are subscribed in the background.
- **Opt-outs still win.** Anyone who unsubscribed or was suppressed stays not subscribed, whatever you confirm.
- **HubSpot's own signals are honored.** A contact marked **Unsubscribed from all email** in HubSpot is unsubscribed in Nitrosend. An address HubSpot has seen hard bounce, or has quarantined, is never subscribed.
- **It covers one HubSpot account.** Nitrosend records who confirmed and when. Reconnecting to a different HubSpot account clears the confirmation.
- **You can withdraw it.** Withdrawing only affects contacts that arrive afterward. Anyone already subscribed stays subscribed.

Contacts that were already in Nitrosend keep the subscription status they have. Until you confirm, you can still use HubSpot contacts in segments and send them transactional email.

Moving your marketing email from HubSpot to Nitrosend? Follow the [migration guide](/guides/migrating-to-nitrosend#hubspot), which brings across only the people who opted in.

## Connect HubSpot

1. Go to **Integrations** in Nitrosend.
2. Choose **HubSpot**.
3. Click **Connect** and complete the HubSpot OAuth flow.
4. Return to Nitrosend and run **Sync contacts**.

The sync runs in the background. The sync progress tray keeps you updated anywhere in the dashboard, or return to the integration detail page for details. Run **Sync contacts** again any time to refresh.

## After import

- Review imported lists in **Contacts > Lists** ([app.nitrosend.com/my/contact-lists](https://app.nitrosend.com/my/contact-lists)).
- Use [segments](/concepts/segments) to build audiences from HubSpot lists and contact fields.
- Send only to contacts who are subscribed in Nitrosend.

<Warning>
  HubSpot sync imports contacts and lists. It does not send campaigns, activate automations, or write data back to HubSpot.
</Warning>
