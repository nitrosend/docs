# Outreach

Outreach finds new leads, verifies that their email addresses are real, and activates them into automated sequences. It is the outbound counterpart to your contact base: contacts are people who know you; leads are people you want to reach.

## How it works

1. **Search** — describe who you want to reach (role, industry, company size, location). Nitrosend runs the search and returns matching leads with a fit rating.
2. **Review** — each lead carries a fit level (high, medium, low) and a deliverability verdict. Reveal a lead to get their verified email address.
3. **Activate** — put revealed leads into a sequence. Activation triggers a flow, so sequences use the same flow engine, templates, and approval gates as everything else you send.
4. **Monitor** — replies land in your agent inbox, and sequence states update live.

## Verification before sending

A lead is only worth contacting if the address is real. Every revealed address is verified before it can enter a sequence, and the deliverability verdict is shown on the lead. Unverified addresses are held back rather than burned against your sending reputation.

## Replies always stop a sequence

The moment a lead replies, their sequence stops. No exceptions, no "one more touch." The reply lands in your inbox queue where your agent or your team picks up the conversation as a human exchange.

## Sequence states

Each lead in a sequence carries one state:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ready | Revealed and eligible for activation |
| Held | Waiting on verification or a safety check |
| In sequence | Actively receiving the sequence |
| Completed | Finished the sequence without a reply |
| Stopped | Sequence ended early, usually by a reply |
| Converted | Replied or took the goal action |
| Failed / Skipped | Could not be contacted, with the reason recorded |

## Outreach vs Campaigns

- **Campaigns** send to your existing audience: contacts who opted in.
- **Outreach** contacts people who have never heard from you. Volume is deliberately paced, addresses are verified first, and replies immediately convert the exchange into a conversation.
