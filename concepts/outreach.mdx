# How Outreach works

Outreach Beta connects lead discovery, research, personal email and follow-ups in one workspace. An outreach can start with existing Contacts, supplied Leads, or a Lead search. Use the [setup guide](/guides/outreach) to run your first outreach.

## Fit, email status and readiness

| What you see | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Fit** | How the person matches your criteria. Missing evidence stays unknown. |
| **Profile facts** | Work details with available sources, such as role, company, and location. |
| **Email status** | Whether an address was checked and what the check found. |
| **Sending readiness** | Whether the person is eligible for this outreach now. Readiness can change after a reply, opt-out, account change or an expired address check. |

Search targets guide work within the approved budget and provider limits. A search may find fewer qualified people than requested. Review the company coverage and held reasons separately from the number of leads saved.

## Follow-ups and replies

You review the people, copy, sending mailbox and schedule before starting. Nitrosend sends the follow-ups you approved and stops the remaining ones for a person when a reply is received. A later ordinary reply or past outreach can appear as history when you review the person again. Explicit opt-outs, suppression and an active outreach remain sending holds.

Gmail permissions cover sending. To handle Gmail replies in Outreach, use a Nitrosend reply Inbox in **Sending**. Each Brand has one included reply Inbox for Outreach. This keeps follow-ups and replies together while continuing to send from the connected address.

## Outreach, Campaigns and Flows

| Use | When to choose it |
| --- | --- |
| **Outreach** | Find or bring your own people, review personal emails, and schedule follow-ups from a connected mailbox. |
| [**Campaigns**](/concepts/campaigns) | Send a newsletter or other one-time email to an eligible audience. |
| [**Flows**](/concepts/flows) | Trigger ongoing automation when a customer signs up, orders or performs another action. |

Leads from discovery stay in Outreach. An address check tells you about the mailbox, while permission to contact the person depends on your relationship and the applicable [Nitrosend Terms](https://nitrosend.com/terms).
