# Pricing

Nitrosend is **pay per send, never per contact** — every plan includes unlimited
contacts. MCP, REST API, CLI, AI copywriting, campaigns, flows, transactional,
templates, and analytics are available on every plan, including Free. The Free
plan starts with non-expiring email credits, then a monthly allowance. No
credit card is required. Read your account's actual terms in Account Billing.

## Plans

The new catalog contains four paid plans: **Starter, Pro, Growth and Scale**,
plus **Free** and negotiated **Enterprise** terms. Monthly and annual base-plan payments share the same
monthly usage allowances. Annual payment is not an annual pool of email credits.

<Info>
  The new catalog is staged, not activated. Its publication status, exact prices,
  included usage, daily sending allowances and add-on terms are generated from
  one commercial source in [PRICING.md](https://nitrosend.com/PRICING.md).
  Existing customers keep their agreed base price. Ordinary packages receive
  current allowances: previous $20 Pro aligns to Starter, and Ultra to Growth.
  Issued credits, earned standing and admitted sends are preserved. Custom
  Enterprise agreements and deliberate operator exceptions remain explicit.
  Account Billing shows the applicable package, price and any account exception.
</Info>

Every brand can prepare one reserved Nitrosend brand-subdomain sender. It does
not consume the customer-domain allowance. Hosted brand subdomains follow the
same plan and standing allowances; they do not have an extra 1,000/day ceiling.
Daily usage is pooled across the account's brands and includes capacity held
for queued or unresolved sends. Email credits are separate from daily capacity.
Provider limits, sender health and pacing still govern actual dispatch.
Each distinct To, CC and BCC recipient counts as one email for credits and
sending capacity, including campaign copies. Duplicate addresses within one
email count once. Buying additional brands, customer domains or seats uses
account-wide capacity; it does not create a separate sending allowance.

Standing combines attributable delivery history, current positive list
verification, domain strength and account signals. Sufficient evidence can
qualify an account on day one; payment alone does not earn trust. Trusted
receives its full plan allowance without a second volume ramp. New sender
contexts still require their first-send safety review, and Probation reviews
each changed content identity. Existing reviewed Enterprise terms remain intact.

List verification is always an explicitly approved operation, never an automatic
step during import, scheduling or sending. For a term that includes monthly
checks, those are used first; prepaid credit pays for the remainder. Existing
operations keep their approved inclusion and rate. The quote shows the included
checks and maximum charge before approval. Buying credit alone does not raise
standing.

Use **Contacts → Verify list** ([app.nitrosend.com/my/contacts](https://app.nitrosend.com/my/contacts)) to choose all contacts in the Brand, a contact
list, a segment, or selected contacts. Review the exact audience and maximum
charge before confirming. Each operation accepts up to 100,000 candidates;
larger audiences must be split into bounded lists or segments. Valid results
can raise standing; invalid, catch-all and unknown results do not prove list
quality. Buying prepaid credits alone does not raise standing.

The dashboard warns at 80% and at exhaustion. Free accounts receive the valid
plan-upgrade action; accounts whose list evidence can improve receive **Verify
your list**. API and MCP capacity responses expose the same reasons and actions.
Queued campaigns wait for capacity; API callers should follow the returned
retry time rather than repeatedly retrying or treating the refusal as delivered.

- **All paid plans** add BYO sending/AI keys, frontier AI models, removable
  branding, and priority support.
- **Scale and Enterprise** can request a separately provisioned dedicated IP;
  paying for a base plan does not automatically provision one.
- **Enterprise** provides negotiated volume, capacity and service terms. Confirm
  any SLA, support or security requirements in the actual agreement.

Qualifying Trusted senders earn extra recurring included emails after clean
sending history and account age meet the catalog's reward terms. The reward
does not increase the starter grant or daily sending limit. There are no
bounce or complaint surcharges; existing safety protections still apply.

See the full feature comparison on the [pricing page](https://nitrosend.com/pricing).

Plans and prepaid balance are separate purchase paths:

- Use a plan checkout to change the recurring subscription and entitlements.
- Add prepaid funds for explicitly approved services such as list verification
  and contact enrichment.

The `nitro_manage_billing` MCP tool supports both paths without paying on the
agent's behalf. Billing mutations require an account-scoped API key or an
authenticated owner/admin handoff. The tool returns an approval URL when
required and a purchase ID for verified status polling. Follow
[Payments for agents](/guides/agent-payments) for MCP, CLI, and REST examples,
or read [Purchase lifecycle](/concepts/payment-lifecycle) for the state and
authority model.

## Lead and contact enrichment

Add missing professional and company details to contacts you already have.
Select contacts and choose **Enrich**, or open a contact and choose **Enrich**.
Review the price and maximum charge before confirming. This uses prepaid funds,
not your included emails. Already-current contacts, no matches and failed
enrichments are not charged.

Enrichment and list verification do different jobs: enrichment adds details;
verification checks email addresses. Your account's quote shows the available
service and actual price. New tier-specific enrichment rates are not activated.

## Inbox capacity

Inbox is in private beta. Included and paid inbox capacity follows your account's
actual contract and the terms in [PRICING.md](https://nitrosend.com/PRICING.md).
Capacity is shared across brands and domains; there is no requirement to upgrade
to Pro just to buy an additional inbox. Existing included capacity is preserved
without stacking it with plan inclusion. Enterprise follows its agreed terms.

Each new email address is a separate inbox. Several inboxes can use the same domain without another domain slot. A new domain does not add an included inbox. Customer-added aliases become included inboxes; old addresses retained after a rename remain receiving routes to that inbox.

Each inbox includes 1 GiB of storage. The optional US$5/month storage upgrade raises one inbox to 10 GiB total. Storage and outbound sending allowances remain separate from inbox capacity; there is no additional incoming-message charge.

Approve the recurring price in Billing before adding paid capacity. Archive unused inboxes before reducing it. If payment ends, extra inboxes keep receiving but cannot reply until capacity is restored.
