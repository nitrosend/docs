# Pricing

Nitrosend is **pay per send, never per contact** — every plan includes unlimited
contacts. MCP, REST API, CLI, AI copywriting, campaigns, flows, transactional,
templates, and analytics are available on every plan, including Free. The Free
plan includes **8,000 free emails up front**, then **500 free emails every
month**. No credit card required.

  Looking for full, programmatic pricing (enterprise volume tiers, overage

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Emails / month | Brands | Customer domains | Rolling 24h recipients | Seats |
|------|-------|----------------|--------|------------------|------------------------|-------|
| **Free** | $0 | 8,000 starter budget, then 500/mo | 3 | 1 | 50 | 1 |
| **Pro** | $20/mo | 20,000 | 5 | 3 | 10,000 | 3 |
| **Ultra** | $100/mo | 125,000 | 10 | 10 | 62,500 | 10 |
| **Enterprise** | from $300/mo | 500K–4M+ (tiered) | Unlimited | Unlimited | Contracted | Unlimited |

Every brand can prepare one reserved Nitrosend brand-subdomain sender. It does
not consume the customer-domain allowance. Brand-subdomain senders have a
separate 1,000-recipient rolling 24-hour infrastructure ceiling, and every
sender also follows its own volume-based capacity and delivery pacing. The
binding limit is always the lowest applicable commercial, sender, provider, or
pacing control. Included email volume never guarantees immediate dispatch
speed.

Email validation has no included allowance on any plan. It is an explicit
prepaid operation and is never started automatically during import, scheduling,
or sending.

- **Pro and above** add BYO sending/AI keys, frontier AI models, removable
  branding, and priority support.
- **Ultra and Enterprise** add dedicated IPs as a paid add-on and more brands,
  seats, and domains. New identities build capacity from clean delivered
  volume.
- **Enterprise** adds SSO / SAML, a 99.9% SLA, a dedicated account manager, and
  tiered volume pricing for higher-volume senders.

See the full feature comparison on the [pricing page](https://nitrosend.com/pricing).
