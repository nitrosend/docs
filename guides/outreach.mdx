# Get started with Outreach

Use Outreach to find people, research their work, and send a short series of personal emails from a mailbox you already use. Decide who to contact, review the emails and schedule, then start sending. When Nitrosend receives a reply, it stops that person's remaining automatic follow-ups.

## Get access to Outreach Beta

Open [Outreach](https://app.nitrosend.com/my/outreach) in the sidebar. New to Nitrosend? [Create an account to get to Outreach Beta](https://app.nitrosend.com/join?redirect_to=%2Fmy%2Foutreach%3Fenable%3D1). If your account has not been invited yet, choose **Join the waitlist**. Tell us who you plan to reach. If you have already applied, the page shows your request status.

Once approved, select your Brand and choose **Review and enable**. On eligible monthly Stripe plans, Outreach costs **US$30 per Brand per month**. The confirmation shows the exact change to your next invoice, including any billing adjustment for the current month. The charge appears on your existing Nitrosend bill.

If you need a paid plan first, choose a plan and return to Outreach to review the add-on. Annual and partner-billed accounts can see their available options in [Account Billing](https://app.nitrosend.com/my/account/billing). Only someone with billing permissions can confirm a charge. Signing up and joining the waitlist are free.

To turn off a Brand's add-on, select that Brand in **Account Billing**, then choose **Disable for this Brand**. Review the invoice adjustment before confirming. Sending pauses for that Brand and your drafts, replies and history stay available.

Lead searches, address checks, enrichment and managed AI show an estimate or price where applicable. [See what's included](/pricing#outreach-beta).

## Connect an email account

In Outreach, choose **Email accounts** → **Connect email account**. Connect Gmail or Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or an SMTP mailbox. A connection makes the address available. Choose the sending address for each outreach in its **Sending** tab.

Each Brand gets one included Nitrosend reply Inbox for Outreach when it first sets up sending or creates a draft. This Inbox is separate from the Brand's sending address. You can use an eligible existing Nitrosend Inbox instead.

**Gmail stays send-only.** With an active Nitrosend reply Inbox selected in **Sending**, new Gmail outreach emails can direct replies there and use automatic follow-ups. Check bounces and vacation responses in Gmail. Without an available reply Inbox, Gmail sends the first email only, and you check replies in Gmail. Optional forwarding for replies sent directly to Gmail is under **Email accounts**. Set up the rule in Gmail and review what it will copy before turning it on.

## Add people or find leads

From Outreach, choose **New outreach**, name it and open **People**. Add existing Contacts, choose saved Leads or import a file. You can start here with people you already know.

To find new people, choose **Find leads** in **People**, or open **Lead searches** from the Outreach workspace. Set the role, location, industry or company requirements. If you need people from specific companies, make those company domains required. Domains supplied as starting points help guide the search.

1. Choose **Estimate lead search** and review the qualified lead target, included allowance and maximum charge.
2. Choose **Find leads** to start at those terms or **Save as draft** to return later.
3. Review the people saved, the company coverage and any reasons results were held. A search can finish below its target when sources lack matching profiles or usable addresses. You can revise the criteria and get a new estimate.

Qualified Leads accepted into the search count toward the allowance, including eligible Leads reused from your Brand. People the search rejects or holds do not incur a qualified lead charge. Usage above your included allowance is capped by the approved quote. Any unused funding hold is released as the search settles.

## Check and enrich your list

Open a person to review profile facts and their sources. Use **Enrich** where available to look up missing work information. An unknown field can stay unknown if sources do not support a result. Check the email separately. A relevant person can still have an unchecked, undeliverable or unknown address.

From **People** or **Review & start**, use the address check action for people who need it. Check the quote before confirming. Catch-all addresses are held by default. A Brand can allow eligible catch-alls at a limited pace under **Sending**. Opt-outs and other sending holds still apply.

## Write, review and start

Open **Emails** to write shared copy and add follow-ups. Open **Personalize** to edit one person's copy. Use an explicit default for optional details, such as a company name, and preview the rendered email. Use **Preview & test** to check a message at your own address before starting.

In **Sending**, select your email account, confirm where replies go and choose the schedule and limits. Open and click tracking are off by default. Select **Review & start** to see who is ready, why people are held, what they'll receive and how they'll be sent. Choose **Start sending** when you're ready.

## Handle replies

Open **Results** to see what sent, what is waiting and what stopped. Open **Replies** to see replies received by Nitrosend and continue the conversation in Inbox. You can respond through the original connected sender. A received reply stops pending automatic follow-ups for that person. An opt-out also blocks later outreach. Prior outreach or an ordinary reply may show a history warning the next time you review the person.

If access expires or sending pauses, you can still see saved outreach and replies. Check the current status in **Account Billing** and review your draft before resuming.

## Use Outreach responsibly

Use Outreach for relevant, permitted email under the [Nitrosend Terms](https://nitrosend.com/terms). A search match, verified address or imported record does not establish permission to contact a person. Use accurate sender details and honour opt-outs. See [Sending Emails](/guides/sending-emails) for sending requirements and [Using the Agent Inbox](/guides/agent-inbox) for reply handling.
