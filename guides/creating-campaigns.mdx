# Creating Campaigns

A campaign is a one-time email or SMS send to an explicit audience.

## Steps

1. **Choose a name** — for internal tracking
2. **Select a template** — pick an existing template or create one inline
3. **Pick your audience** — choose contact lists, a segment, or All Contacts
4. **Preview and test** — send a test email to yourself
5. **Send or schedule** — send immediately or pick a date and time

## Audience options

| Audience | Use when |
|----------|----------|
| Contact lists | You want to send to one or more curated lists. Lists can also be filtered by a segment. |
| Segment | You want to send to contacts matching saved rules, without choosing a list first. |
| All Contacts | You intentionally want to send to every subscribed contact in the brand. |

If no audience is selected, the campaign stays a draft and delivery is blocked. All Contacts requires a confirmation before sending or scheduling.

## Campaign states

| State | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Draft | Being edited, not yet sent |
| Scheduled | Queued for a future send time |
| Sending | Currently being delivered |
| Sent | Delivery complete |

## Tracking

After sending, the **Activity** page in the sidebar lists recent campaigns with their sent count and open and click rates, and each contact's activity timeline shows their recorded opens and clicks. Ask your agent for a campaign's bounces and unsubscribes. Open and click tracking can be turned off per brand; see [Open and click tracking](/guides/sending-emails#open-and-click-tracking).

## Test subject lines

When a campaign goes to 1,000 people or more, the send review can test other subject lines against yours. NitroLLM has some suggested alternatives ready, each taking a different approach and using only what's in your email. Edit or remove any of them, or write your own. Your own subject line is always version A.

- **Split everyone** is the default. Each person gets one line at random. Two days after the last email goes out, the campaign page shows how each line did and whether one clearly won.
- **Send the winner** gives the test to part of the list. After 2, 4, 8 or 24 hours, the line with the most opens (or clicks, if you choose) goes to everyone else. If no line clearly won, the rest gets your own line.

How many lines you can test follows the audience: two from 1,000 recipients, three from 2,500, five from 5,000 and six from 20,000. When you send the winner, the test part follows the same table, and at least 5% of the list is held back for the winner.

Rates stay hidden until the result is decided, because opens keep arriving after a send. Then each line shows its open, click and unsubscribe rates as they stood at that moment, so later opens never change a result. A line wins only when it beats every other line by more than chance would explain. Under Safe Mode, the account owner approves the whole set of subject lines before anything sends.

Agents send versions with `nitro_control_delivery` (`versions`, and `winner` to send the winner). `nitro_review_delivery` returns the suggested lines in `preflight.versions`, with the call that sends them.

## Iterate on a sent campaign

Sent campaigns are locked — their content, audience, schedule, and template can't be changed so the record stays faithful to what went out. To send a variant, click **Edit as new campaign** on the locked banner (or **Duplicate** from the overflow menu on any row). You get a fresh draft that inherits the audience and email but resets the schedule and approval, ready for you to tweak and re-send.

Renaming a sent campaign still works on any status — the name is internal metadata.

See [Campaigns → Edit lock and lifecycle](/concepts/campaigns#edit-lock-and-lifecycle) for the full matrix.
