# Partner program

Nitrosend partners earn **40% of every payment from each client they refer**,
on any plan, for as long as that client stays with Nitrosend. Every partner
account also includes **client management**, so you can set up Nitrosend
accounts for your clients.

<Info>
  The full rules are in the [affiliate terms](https://nitrosend.com/affiliate-terms).
  If anything on this page differs from the terms, the terms apply.
</Info>

## What you earn

You earn 40% commission on every client you refer, on Pro, Ultra or Enterprise.
There is no 12-month cap: commission continues for as long as the client stays
with Nitrosend.

At list price, 40% is $8 a month for a client on Pro ($20/mo), $40 a month on
Ultra ($100/mo), and from $120 a month on Enterprise (from $300/mo). See
[Pricing](/pricing) for the plans.

## Keeping 40%

You start at 40%. To keep it, bring in one new paying client every 6 months.

- Your first 6 months start the day you join. If you joined before 7 October
  2026, they start on 7 October 2026, so you're at 40% until at least 7 April
  2027.
- Each new client's first payment restarts your 6 months once it has cleared
  the 30-day refund window.
- If 6 months pass without a new paying client, your rate eases to **20%**.
- Your next paying referral takes **all** your clients straight back to 40%.

## Your Nitrosend plan

Joining the partner program doesn't change your Nitrosend plan or its price.
Your Affiliates tab, client management and commissions work on any plan,
including Free.

**Partners who joined before 7 October 2026** have **$20 off every month for 12
months from 7 October 2026**, on Pro, Ultra or Enterprise. On Pro, that makes
the plan free for the year.

- No card is needed while the discount covers your plan.
- To keep a paid plan after the 12 months, add a card in your account before
  the discount ends. Without one, your account moves to the Free plan at the end
  of that billing period.
- Sending beyond your plan's monthly allowance also needs a card on file.
  Without one, sending pauses at the allowance until you add a card.

## Client management

Client management is switched on for every partner account. Create Nitrosend
accounts for your clients and set them up before the client is invited.

- **Clients page**: create and manage client accounts at
  [`https://app.nitrosend.com/my/clients`](https://app.nitrosend.com/my/clients).
- **Partner API**: create and manage client accounts from your own systems. Set
  up credentials under **Clients > API**
  ([`https://app.nitrosend.com/my/clients/api`](https://app.nitrosend.com/my/clients/api)).

The client owns and pays for their account. Clients you create this way earn
you commission like any other referral.

## Find your link

Open the **Affiliates** tab in the Nitrosend app:
[`https://app.nitrosend.com/my/affiliates`](https://app.nitrosend.com/my/affiliates).
Your referral link sits at the top, with your visitors, signups, customers and
earnings under it.

Referrals are tracked with a 60-day cookie: a visitor who clicks your link is
credited to you if they sign up within 60 days.

## Payouts

Referrals and commission are tracked in Rewardful.

- Commission becomes payable once the client's payment has cleared the
  **30-day refund window**. Refunds and chargebacks inside that window reverse
  the commission.
- Payable commission is paid out **monthly**, via **PayPal or Wise**.
