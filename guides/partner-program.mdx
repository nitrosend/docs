# Partner program

Nitrosend partners earn **40% of every payment from each client they refer**,
on any plan, for as long as that client stays with Nitrosend. Partners also get
**$20 off Nitrosend every month for their first year**, and can set up
Nitrosend accounts for their clients.

<Info>
  The full rules are in the [affiliate terms](https://nitrosend.com/affiliate-terms).
  If anything on this page differs from the terms, the terms apply.
</Info>

## What you earn

You earn 40% commission on every client you refer, on Pro, Ultra or Enterprise.
There is no 12-month cap: commission continues for as long as the client stays
with Nitrosend.

At list price, 40% is $8 a month for a client on Pro ($20/mo), $40 a month on
Ultra ($100/mo), and from $120 a month on Enterprise (from $300/mo). See
[Pricing](/pricing) for the plans.

## Keeping 40%

You start at 40% when you join. To keep it, bring in one new paying client
every 6 months.

- If 6 months pass without a new paying client, your rate eases to **20%**.
- Your next paying referral takes **all** your clients straight back to 40%.

## $20 off Nitrosend

Partner accounts are on Nitrosend Pro with **$20 off every month for 12
months**, so Pro is free for your first year. The $20 also comes off Ultra or
Enterprise if you move up.

You don't need a card to start. After the 12 months, add a card to stay on a
paid plan.

## Client management

Partners can create Nitrosend accounts for their clients and set them up before
the client is invited.

- **Clients page**: create and manage client accounts at
  [`https://app.nitrosend.com/my/clients`](https://app.nitrosend.com/my/clients).
- **Partner API**: create and manage client accounts from your own systems. Set
  up credentials under **Clients > API**
  ([`https://app.nitrosend.com/my/clients/api`](https://app.nitrosend.com/my/clients/api)).

The client owns and pays for their account. Clients you create this way earn
you commission like any other referral.

## Find your link

Open the **Affiliates** tab in the Nitrosend app:
[`https://app.nitrosend.com/my/affiliates`](https://app.nitrosend.com/my/affiliates).
Your referral link sits at the top, with your visitors, signups, customers and
earnings under it.

Referrals are tracked with a 60-day cookie: a visitor who clicks your link is
credited to you if they sign up within 60 days.

## Payouts

Referrals and commission are tracked in Rewardful.

- Commission is paid **monthly**, via **PayPal or Wise**.
- Commission clears once the client's payment is past the **30-day refund
  window**.
